La Cabine Air resumes the project of La Cabine.
La Cabine is a device to collect the public's voice on different subjets.

To do so, the setup of La Cabine is designed as a photo booth box where people enter in to take a seat on a bench to face up the supplies that record their testimonies.
The hardware consists of a camera, two microphones, a ring light, a monitor and a switches angine.
