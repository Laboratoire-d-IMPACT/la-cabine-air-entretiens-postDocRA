La Cabine Air resumes the project of La Cabine.

La Cabine is a device to collect the public's voice on different subjets.

To do so, the setup of La Cabine is designed as a photo booth box where people enter in to take a seat on a bench to face up the supplies that record their testimonies.

The hardware at the disposable of the public consists in a camera, two microphones, a ring light, a monitor and a switches angine.

The technical material wich is concealed to be only reachable by the staff is a Mac mini, a keyboard and a mouse.

In the case of the resarch-action, La Cabine Air uses La Cabine idea.
It is adapted to the employees interviews.
